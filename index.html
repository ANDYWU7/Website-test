A little context

Code is how I
bring ideas to life.

I'm Andy Wu, a computer science student with extensive experience coding and a growing focus on game development. Before turning my attention toward interactive worlds, I spent years building my foundation in programming, learning how to break down complex problems, design clear solutions, and turn abstract ideas into working software.

I'm especially interested in the space where technology and storytelling meet. Game development gives me a way to combine logic with imagination: writing systems that feel responsive, shaping spaces that invite exploration, and creating experiences that communicate something without needing to say it directly.

As I continue learning, I'm building toward a career where I can contribute strong technical thinking, thoughtful design, and a genuine curiosity about how players experience a world. My goal is to make games that invite people to explore—not just a map, but an idea, a feeling, or a different way of seeing the world.
